Publication ethics

COPE-aligned duties for authors, reviewers and editors.

Authors

Submit only original work. Do not send the same manuscript to another journal at the same time. Name every author who made a substantial contribution. Disclose funding, conflicts and the use of generative AI for text or figures. Live-animal and field work needs committee approval and permits.

Reviewers

Decline if you cannot judge the paper or if you have a close conflict. Keep the manuscript confidential. Do not use the ideas before publication.

Editors

Decisions rest on scope, methods and honesty, not on the author’s country or the likelihood of citations. The speed targets (first decision in 1 day; acceptance in 8–10 days) do not replace the need for a documented review. Papers that cannot be reviewed properly in that window should wait, not be rubber-stamped.

Misconduct

Plagiarism, image manipulation, fabricated data or fake reviews lead to rejection or retraction. Contact editor@aquaculture-scientific.br.
