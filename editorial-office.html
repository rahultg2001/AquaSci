Aims and Scope

Aquaculture Scientific is an international, peer-reviewed journal focused on advancing knowledge and innovation in aquaculture, fisheries, and aquatic biology.

It serves as a platform for researchers and professionals to share original research and reviews that support the sustainable management of aquatic resources.

The journal emphasizes high-quality scientific research addressing challenges in aquatic food production, ecosystem health, and biodiversity conservation, with relevance to environmental, economic, and practical aspects.

Scope

Aquaculture Scientific welcomes original research articles, review articles, short communications, technical notes, and other scientifically relevant contributions covering, but not limited to:

  • Aquaculture nutrition and production
  • Fish biology and physiology
  • Reproductive biotechnology
  • Fisheries management
  • Aquatic environment and ecosystems
  • Biodiversity and conservation
  • Aquatic animal pathology
  • Sustainable aquaculture
  • Blue economy

Open Access Policy

Aquaculture Scientific is a fully open-access, peer-reviewed journal. All published articles are freely available online to readers worldwide immediately upon publication, without subscription or paywall barriers.

Article Processing Charge (APC)

To support the costs associated with editorial management, peer review administration, copyediting, typesetting, online publication, website maintenance, and long-term digital preservation, Aquaculture Scientific charges an Article Processing Charge for accepted manuscripts.

The APC is charged only after a manuscript has been accepted for publication.

There are no submission fees and no fees for the initial editorial assessment or peer-review process.

Payment of an APC does not influence the editorial decision, peer-review process, or acceptance of a manuscript.

All manuscripts are evaluated solely on the basis of their scientific quality, originality, relevance, methodological rigor, and compliance with the journal's editorial standards.

APC Waivers and Discounts

Authors who face genuine financial difficulties may contact the editorial office to request a partial or full APC waiver.

Waiver requests will be considered independently and will not influence the editorial evaluation or peer-review decision.

The journal may also provide APC discounts or waivers for invited articles, special issues, early-career researchers, or other cases determined by the Editorial Board.
