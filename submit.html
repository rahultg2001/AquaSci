Privacy and editorial review

Submit only information and files necessary for editorial assessment. Please ensure your documents do not disclose confidential information improperly.

The editorial team will begin an initial assessment as soon as practicable. Review timelines vary according to the manuscript and reviewer availability.

Before you submit

1. Download the official Word template .

2. Follow the relevant article-type guidelines.

3. Include the required ethics and data availability statements.
