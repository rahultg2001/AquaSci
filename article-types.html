Article types

We publish every length: short notes to full papers and book chapters.

All types are accepted, from full research papers to short notes. The table is ordered by weight (1 = highest). Fees are waived 100% until 31 December 2026.

WeightTypeTypical lengthPeer reviewedWhen to use
1Original research4,000–8,000 wordsYesFull experiment or field study

3Review / systematic review6,000–10,000 wordsYesStructured synthesis of a field
4Book chapter4,000–7,000 wordsYesChapter for an AS edited volume
5Methods / protocol2,500–5,000 wordsYesReusable method or husbandry protocol
6Case study2,000–4,500 wordsYesFarm, hatchery, outbreak or policy case
7Data paper1,500–4,000 wordsYesDescribed dataset with a public link
8Short communication1,500–3,000 wordsYesConcise novel result
9Extension / practice paper1,500–3,500 wordsYesOn-farm practice for technicians and producers
10Technical note1,200–2,500 wordsYesEquipment, farm system or analytical tweak
11Perspective / opinion1,500–3,500 wordsEditor + 1 reviewerArgument, agenda or commentary
12Brief report800–1,500 wordsYesSingle result, outbreak note or field observation
13Popular article800–2,000 wordsEditor + 1 reviewerPlain-language piece for farmers, students and the public
14Photo note / illustrated note400–1,000 wordsEditorStrong images with a short scientific caption
