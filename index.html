About the Journal

Aquaculture Scientific is an international, English-language, open-access journal publishing research on aquaculture, capture fisheries, aquatic health, nutrition, genetics, engineering and the communities that depend on them. The journal is published quarterly from Porto Alegre, Brazil. Manuscripts can be submitted online through the manuscript submission page.

The journal welcomes disciplinary, interdisciplinary and applied studies from hatchery to harvest, from pond to policy. We publish original research, reviews, short communications, popular articles, brief notes and book chapters. A 100% APC waiver applies through 31 December 2026.
