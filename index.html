About the journal

Aquaculture Scientific is an international, English-language, open-access journal. We publish every scale of paper — popular articles and brief notes through full research, reviews and book chapters. Fees depend on article type; a 100% waiver applies through 31 December 2026.

The journal welcomes disciplinary, interdisciplinary and applied studies from hatchery to harvest, from pond to policy. Manuscripts are accepted in English only.
