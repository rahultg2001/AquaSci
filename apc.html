How payment works after the waiver window

Invoices can be issued in BRL (Pix or boleto), USD (card or wire) or INR (NEFT/UPI via partner). The corresponding author chooses the currency at acceptance.

Approximate FX used for listing only; the billed amount is locked in the chosen currency.

Waivers after 31 Dec 2026

Authors with no grant support, or corresponding authors based in World Bank low-income economies, may request a partial or full waiver at submission. Requests do not influence the scientific decision.
